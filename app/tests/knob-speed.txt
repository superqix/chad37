# KNOB SPEED (GLOBE twice: the hardware page, K7): how far a knob's value moves a detent, for every knob sending its CC
# in every preset. X1 (the default) moves 1, X2 2, X4 4, X8 8; ACCEL moves 1 turned slowly and up to 6 turned fast.
# The settings pages' own knobs keep 1 a detent. It is saved with the rest.
wait 1200
listen
wait 30
expect knob_speed 0

# X1: K1 (CC 48 ch 6) one a detent
out
spin K1 3
wait 30
expect_out USB B5 30 03

# The hardware page: K7 (ALGO) sets it; its own steps stay 1 a detent
tap GLO
wait 30
tap GLO
wait 30
expect page 5
spin ALGO 1
wait 30
expect knob_speed 1
said X2
shot hw-knob-speed.png
tap HOME
wait 30
expect page 0

# X2: three detents move 6
out
spin K1 3
wait 30
expect_out USB B5 30 09

# X8: a detent moves 8, to the top in 15 detents; K7 on the main view sends its CC
tap GLO
wait 30
tap GLO
wait 30
spin ALGO 2
wait 30
expect knob_speed 3
said X8
tap HOME
wait 30
out
spin K1 1
wait 30
expect_out USB B5 30 11
out
spin K1 20
wait 30
expect_out USB B5 30 7F
out
spin ALGO 2
wait 30
expect_out USB B5 36 10

# ACCEL: slow detents (200 ms apart) move 1, quick ones (20 ms) 6: the first of the quick ones comes after a pause
tap GLO
wait 30
tap GLO
wait 30
spin ALGO 1
wait 30
expect knob_speed 4
said ACCEL
spin ALGO 5
wait 30
expect knob_speed 4
tap HOME
wait 30
spin K2 -127
wait 300
out
turn K2 3 200
wait 30
expect_out USB B5 31 03
out
turn K2 4 20
wait 30
expect_out USB B5 31 16

# Kept over a restart; back to X1
tap SAVE
wait 100
reboot
wait 1200
expect knob_speed 4
tap GLO
wait 30
tap GLO
wait 30
spin ALGO -9
wait 30
expect knob_speed 0
said X1
tap HOME
wait 30
