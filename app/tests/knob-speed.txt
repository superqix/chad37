# KNOB SPEED (GLOBE twice: the hardware page, K7): how far a knob's value moves a detent, for every knob sending its CC
# in every preset. ACCEL (the default) follows the turn's speed: 1 a detent turned slowly, up to 12 spun fast; X1, X2,
# X4 and X8 move 1, 2, 4 and 8. The settings pages' own knobs keep 1 a detent. It is saved with the rest.
# ("spin" turns a knob's detents all at once and exactly, unaccelerated; "turn" spaces them in time, as a hand does.)
wait 1200
listen
wait 30
expect knob_speed 0

# ACCEL, slowly (a detent every 250 ms): K2 (CC 49 ch 6) one a detent, for fine moves
out
turn K2 5 250
wait 30
expect_out USB B5 31 05

# ... spun fast (40 detents a second): it speeds up over a few detents and sweeps to the top in 20
out
turn K2 20 25
wait 30
expect_out USB B5 31 7F

# ... and turned back slowly, one a detent again
out
turn K2 -3 250
wait 30
expect_out USB B5 31 7C

# ... at 10 detents a second, about twice a detent once under way: 10 detents move 18
out
turn K3 10 100
wait 30
expect_out USB B5 32 12

# The hardware page: K7 (ALGO) sets it; its own steps stay 1 a detent
tap GLO
wait 30
tap GLO
wait 30
expect page 5
turn ALGO 1 20
wait 30
expect knob_speed 1
said X1
tap HOME
wait 30
expect page 0

# X1: K1 (CC 48) one a detent, however fast
out
turn K1 6 20
wait 30
expect_out USB B5 30 06

# X2: three detents move 6
tap GLO
wait 30
tap GLO
wait 30
spin ALGO 1
wait 30
expect knob_speed 2
said X2
shot hw-knob-speed.png
tap HOME
wait 30
out
spin K1 3
wait 30
expect_out USB B5 30 0C

# X8: a detent moves 8, to the top in 15 detents; K7 on the main view sends its CC
tap GLO
wait 30
tap GLO
wait 30
spin ALGO 2
wait 30
expect knob_speed 4
said X8
tap HOME
wait 30
out
spin K1 1
wait 30
expect_out USB B5 30 14
out
spin K1 20
wait 30
expect_out USB B5 30 7F
out
spin ALGO 2
wait 30
expect_out USB B5 36 10

# Kept over a restart; back to ACCEL, the first choice
tap SAVE
wait 100
reboot
wait 1200
expect knob_speed 4
tap GLO
wait 30
tap GLO
wait 30
spin ALGO -9
wait 30
expect knob_speed 0
said ACCEL
tap HOME
wait 30
