# 16 chord-pad banks shared by the presets: < and > pick one in CHORD and EASY CHORD, < + > asks to delete it and
# < + > again deletes. Holding PATCH lights pads 1-8 by preset: current white, in use red, unused green.
wait 1200
ble connect
listen
wait 30

# The first start with banks: presets 1-8's pad chords become banks 1-8, the active preset's bank active
expect banks_from_presets 1
expect bank 1
expect bank_used 0

# CHORD: assign C Maj to pad 1 of bank 1
tap ENV
wait 30
expect chord_on 1
keybed 12 down
keybed 16 down
keybed 19 down
wait 30
keybed 12 up
keybed 16 up
keybed 19 up
wait 30
tap REC
wait 30
tappad 0 100
wait 60
expect pad_chord0 3
expect bank_used 1
out

# > : bank 2, empty; the arrow sends no Program Change in chord modes
tap RIGHT
wait 30
expect bank 2
expect bank_used 0
expect pad_chord0 0
expect program 0
said BANK
tappad 0 100
wait 60
expect active_notes 0

# Assign D Min (D4 F4 A4: keys 14 17 21) to pad 2 of bank 2
keybed 14 down
keybed 17 down
keybed 21 down
wait 30
keybed 14 up
keybed 17 up
keybed 21 up
wait 30
tap REC
wait 30
tappad 1 100
wait 60
expect pad_chord1 3
expect bank_used 1
out

# < : bank 1 again, pad 1 still plays C Maj on the key channel
tap LEFT
wait 30
expect bank 1
expect pad_chord0 3
expect pad_chord1 0
tappad 0 100
wait 60
expect_out USB 90 3C 64
expect active_notes 0
out

# Ends stop: < at bank 1 stays on 1; > runs up to 16 and stays

wait 30

out
