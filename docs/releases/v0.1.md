# Chad37 0.1

The first release of Chad37: chord-pad MIDI controller firmware for the M-Vave SMK-37. It turns the keyboard into a
pure MIDI controller with clean, predictable controls, plus chord, scale and arpeggiator modes.

**To install:** open the [web installer](https://superqix.github.io/chad37/) in Chrome or Edge, or download `fm1_install.py` and your model's
package below and follow the [install guide](https://github.com/superqix/chad37/blob/main/docs/install.md). The
[user guide](https://github.com/superqix/chad37/blob/main/docs/manual.md) covers every control.

| Your keyboard | Package | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| SMK-37 Elite | `chad37-smk37-elite-0.1.fwsc` | In daily use |
| SMK-37 Pro | `chad37-smk37-0.1.fwsc` | Tested by a user |
| MKE-P37 | `chad37-smk37-mke-0.1.fwsc` | Never installed: experimental |

You can return to M-Vave's firmware at any time from [open.m-vave.com](https://open.m-vave.com).

## What's in it

- **Controls that behave.** Every knob sends an absolute 0–127 (stock's K7–K16 were relative, which synths read as a
  switch), and the faders are on free CCs 20–27 (stock put fader 1 on the sustain pedal).
- **Fast, wired or wireless.** USB, Bluetooth MIDI (SMK-37 Elite) and the TRS MIDI jack, all at once. Bluetooth is
  tested with Windows, macOS and iOS, and keeps up with the cable.
- **CHORD mode.** Play a chord, name it on screen, and put it on a pad. 16 banks of 16 pads, shared by all presets,
  with pad erase, latch, voicings and strum. Chord pads can have their own MIDI channel (for Surge XT's Channel Split).
- **Easy Chord.** Full chords from single keys.
- **SCALE mode.** Pick a key and scale: the white keys stay in it, and the pads fill with its chords.
- **Arpeggiator.** Five modes, four rates, three octaves, MIDI clock sync, tap tempo, hold, swing, and a 16-step
  rhythm of notes, accents and rests tapped in on the pads.
- **The screen.** A big dial or fader as you move a control, a chord view, and 8 presets you can see while holding
  PATCH.
